# Project Positioning

Chinese original: [00_project_positioning.md](./00_project_positioning.md)

Open Pill Reminder is positioned as an offline-first local reminder device.

Key statements:

- It is not a medical device
- It does not provide medical advice
- It does not determine medicine type, dosage, or efficacy
- The first version focuses only on local reminder, local confirmation, and local record storage

The product should remain small in scope, easy to understand, and reliable in local use.
